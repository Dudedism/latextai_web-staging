We're excited to announce that **LaTexT** is officially live!

LaTexT is an AI-powered service that converts your Word documents into professionally formatted LaTeX PDFs. Whether you're submitting to a journal, preparing a conference paper, or just want beautiful typesetting, LaTexT handles the heavy lifting.

## How it works

1. **Upload** your Word document (.docx)
2. **Choose** your target journal template
3. **Pay** with credits or a one-time payment
4. **Download** your professionally formatted LaTeX PDF

Our pipeline handles figures, tables, equations, citations, and all the formatting details that make LaTeX submissions painful to do by hand.

## What's next

We're continuously improving our conversion pipeline and adding support for more journal templates. Stay tuned for updates!
